01 Your role as owner

BakiBondhu (বাকিবন্ধু) is a Bangla-first, offline-first credit-ledger app for Bangladeshi shopkeepers. Each shopkeeper installs the Android app, records their customers' বাকি (credit) and পরিশোধ (payments) and daily sales, and — once they create an account — their data syncs to the cloud.

As the owner, you sit above every shop. You don't use the shopkeeper app to manage the platform; you use a separate, private web Admin Panel. From there you can:

  • See platform-wide numbers — how many shops, users, customers, and how much credit is outstanding across everyone.
  • Manage each shop's subscription — set, extend, or clear an expiry date.
  • Suspend a shop temporarily, or delete one permanently.
  • Clean up data — remove a customer, clear a shop's sales, reset a user's password.
  • Review an audit log of every action you take.

ℹ️ The panel is web-only

The Admin Panel is not inside the downloadable app. It lives at a private web address and only your owner account can log in. Ordinary shopkeepers never see it.

02 Before you start

You need three things:

1 · The panel address

https://bakibondhu.infinityfreeapp.com/admin

2 · Your admin login

Username Admin and the password you chose during setup.

3 · A web browser

Any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, or Firefox — on a phone or computer.

Where your admin account came from

Your owner account was created once, during first-time setup, through a special page (admin_setup.php). That page was then removed for safety, so it can never be used again to create another admin. This is by design — there is exactly one admin account, and it is yours.

⚠️ Keep these safe

Your admin password is the master key to the whole platform. Store it in a password manager, never share it, and never type it on a computer you don't trust. If you ever forget it, recovery requires developer access to the server database — so don't lose it.

03 Signing in to the panel

  1. Open your browser and go to https://bakibondhu.infinityfreeapp.com/admin
  2. You'll see the বাকিবন্ধু · Admin sign-in box. Enter your Username (Admin) and Password.
  3. Click Sign in. The dashboard opens on the Overview tab.

Across the top you'll always have three tabs — Overview, Shops, Audit log — plus ↻ Refresh and Log out.

ℹ️ Sessions last 8 hours

For safety, an admin sign-in stays valid for about 8 hours, then you'll be asked to sign in again. On a shared or public computer, always click Log out when you're done.

✓ Wrong password?

If you see "wrong username or password", check Caps Lock and that the username is exactly Admin (capital A). The panel briefly runs a background security check the first time it loads — if a page looks blank for a second, just wait or refresh once.

06 Subscriptions & trials

Every shop has an expiry date — the day its subscription runs through. You control it from the Set expiry button.

The 30-day free trial

When a new shopkeeper registers, the app automatically gives them a 30-day trial: their expiry is set to 30 days from sign-up. They can use everything immediately, and you decide what happens when the trial ends.

Setting or extending an expiry

  1. On the Shops tab, find the shop and click Set expiry.
  2. Pick the date the subscription should run through, using the date picker.
  3. Click Save. The Expiry column updates immediately.

To give a shop unlimited access (no expiry), open Set expiry and click Make unlimited instead.

What the shop experiences

  • The shop stays fully active through the expiry day, and is blocked starting the next day.
  • Once expired, when the shopkeeper tries to log in or sync, they're blocked and shown a Bangla message: "আপনার সাবস্ক্রিপশনের মেয়াদ শেষ হয়ে গেছে। অ্যাডমিনের সাথে যোগাযোগ করুন।" (Your subscription has expired — contact the admin.)
  • Their data is not deleted — the moment you extend the date, access returns.

✓ In-app reminder

Shopkeepers on v0.1.7 or newer see a banner on their Home screen as the expiry gets close (within the last week) and once it passes — nudging them to renew before they're locked out.

ℹ️ Expiry vs. suspend

Expiry is for normal billing — it lapses on a date and the shopkeeper is prompted to renew. Suspend (section 7) is a manual, immediate block for cases like abuse. Both stop login and sync; expiry does it on a schedule, suspend does it instantly.

07 Suspend & unsuspend a shop

Suspending is an immediate on/off switch for a shop's access — useful if a shop is misusing the service or you need to freeze it while you sort something out.

  1. On the Shops tab, find the shop and click Suspend.
  2. Confirm the prompt. The shop's Status flips to suspended.
  3. To restore it, click Unsuspend on the same row.

While suspended, that shop's users cannot log in and cannot sync — they'll see an "account suspended; contact support" message. Nothing is deleted; unsuspending returns them to normal.

08 Deleting a shop

Deleting removes a shop and everything in it — its users, customers, transactions, and sales. Use it to clear out test shops or a shop that asked to be removed.

  1. On the Shops tab, click Delete on the shop's row.
  2. Read the confirmation carefully — it names the shop and warns this cannot be undone.
  3. Confirm. The shop disappears and the action is logged.

⛔ This cannot be undone

There is no "trash" or restore. Once you confirm, the shop and all of its data are gone from the cloud for good. When in doubt, suspend instead of delete, or export the shop's records first (the shopkeeper can export a customer's history from the app).

⚠️ Deletes reach the phone only on reinstall

Deleting on the server removes the shop from the cloud and the panel. If that shop is still installed and logged in on a phone, its local copy stays on that phone until the app is reinstalled — deletions don't push back down to a device.

09 Customers, sales & data cleanup

Click View on any shop to open its detail. You'll see three parts:

  • Users — the shop's accounts, with Reset password and Delete (see section 10).
  • Customers & balances — every customer with phone, address, transaction count and current balance. Each has a Delete button.
  • Direct sales — the count of sale records, with a Clear all sales button.

Delete a single customer

In the Customers & balances table, click Delete on that customer's row and confirm. This removes the customer and all of their transactions from the cloud. Handy for pruning junk or test entries.

Clear a shop's sales

Under Direct sales, Clear all sales deletes every sale record for that shop (it does not touch customers or the credit ledger). Good for resetting a demo shop before launch.

Export every customer to Excel

The ⬇ Download customers button in the panel's top bar downloads a single Excel (.xlsx) workbook of every customer across every shop, grouped by shop owner — a Summary sheet (one row per shop: owner, phone, customer count, total due, status, expiry) plus an All customers sheet you can filter or pivot. Nothing is changed; it's read-only. Handy for a platform-wide dues report.

⚠️ These are permanent too

Deleting a customer or clearing sales cannot be undone, and — like shop delete — applies to the cloud copy. A device still logged into that shop keeps its local copy until reinstalled.

10 Shop users & passwords

Inside a shop's View, the Users section lists that shop's login accounts.

Reset a user's password

  1. Open the shop with View.
  2. In the Users table, click Reset password on the user's row.
  3. Type a new password (at least 6 characters) and confirm.
  4. Tell the shopkeeper their new password so they can log in and change it later.

Use this when a shopkeeper is locked out and can't remember their password.

Delete a user

Delete on a user removes just that login account. To remove the whole shop and its data, use Delete on the Shops tab instead (section 8).

ℹ️ Shopkeeper mobile numbers are unique

Each shop-owner registers with a unique mobile number (it's their login ID), and within any one shop, customers can't share a mobile number either. If a shopkeeper says "it won't accept my number," it's likely already registered.

11 The audit log

The Audit log tab shows the latest 50 actions taken from the Admin Panel — each with the time, the admin (you), the action, and what it applied to.

Actions recorded include: suspend_business, unsuspend_business, delete_business, set_expiry, delete_user, reset_password, delete_customer and clear_sales.

✓ Use it as your memory

Before you worry "did I already extend that shop?" or "when did I suspend them?", check the audit log — it's the reliable record of every change you've made.

12 Everyday playbook

Common situations and exactly what to do:

A new shop just signed up

Nothing to do — they get a 30-day trial automatically. Watch them in Overview and Shops. Near day 30, decide: extend (they're paying) or let it lapse.

A shop paid you — extend their subscription

Shops → Set expiry → pick the new date (e.g. one month or one year ahead) → Save. Access continues seamlessly.

A shop stopped paying

Easiest: do nothing — the expiry lapses on its date and they're prompted to renew. Their data is kept, so they return the moment they pay and you extend. For an immediate cut-off, Suspend them.

A shopkeeper is locked out / forgot their password

View the shop → Users → Reset password → give them the new one (section 10).

Clearing out test data before launch

  1. In Shops, delete any obviously fake shops (test names, dummy phone numbers, all-zero stats) with Delete.
  2. For a real shop that has junk mixed in, View it and Delete the junk customers and/or Clear all sales.
  3. Check Overview — the totals should now reflect only real activity.

Checking who owes the most across the platform

Sort by eye using the Outstanding column on the Shops tab, or open a shop's View — its customers are listed with balances, highest first.

13 Security & good practice

  • One admin, one owner. There is a single admin account. Treat its password like the keys to a safe.
  • Use a strong, unique password and store it in a password manager — not on paper by the till.
  • Log out on shared devices. Sessions expire after ~8 hours, but always Log out when finished on a computer others use.
  • Prefer suspend over delete when you're unsure — delete is permanent.
  • Double-check the name in every confirmation dialog before deleting a shop, customer, or sales.
  • Never enter your admin password into any page that isn't the real panel at bakibondhu.infinityfreeapp.com/admin.

⚠️ Hosting note

The platform currently runs on a free shared-hosting plan. It works, but free hosting can be slower or have downtime, and has usage limits. As you grow, plan to move to a paid, reliable host — your developer can advise on the migration.

14 The mobile & web apps

Shopkeepers use the Android app. They can start using it offline without an account; to sync to the cloud (and appear in your panel), they log in or register.

Where shopkeepers get the app

From your landing page — https://bakibondhu.infinityfreeapp.com — which has a big অ্যাপ ডাউনলোড করুন (Download app) button. The download always points to the latest version, so the link never goes stale.

Updating to a new version

  • New versions are built and published by your developer. The current version is v0.1.22. Shopkeepers are prompted in-app when a newer version is available (Settings → Update app); updating keeps all their data.
  • To update, a shopkeeper simply downloads and installs the newer APK over the old one — their data is preserved and the local database upgrades automatically.
  • Server-side rules (like subscription expiry) apply to all versions, so even an older app is protected; only new in-app features (location dropdowns at signup, search, sort, export, the expiry banner) require updating.

ℹ️ What shopkeepers can do in the app

Add customers (choosing their location by division → district → thana at signup), record credit & payments, log daily sales, send SMS/WhatsApp reminders, track collection activities & payment promises, and — in recent versions — search, sort, edit customers, delete a customer once they're fully paid, and export a customer's transaction history. As of v0.1.17 everything (including collections & promises) syncs to the cloud, so it stays the same on their phone and on the web. Each shopkeeper only ever sees their own shop's customers, even on a shared phone. Their expiry and account are managed by you from the panel.

Newer additions shopkeepers may ask about: a Bangla / English language toggle (top of the login screen and in Settings — v0.1.21); a one-tap Download of all their customers as an Excel (.xlsx) file to the phone's Downloads folder (from Settings or the Home screen — v0.1.22); and an in-app Update prompt. Since v0.1.20 the app requires a shop-owner account — there is no "use without an account" option, so every user you see in the panel signed up properly.

The web app — same ledger, in a browser

Shopkeepers don't have to install anything: the same account works in any browser at https://bakibondhu.infinityfreeapp.com/app. It's a full version of the app — customers, credit & payments, reminders, collections, sales — and because it shares the same cloud database, whatever they do on the web shows on their phone and vice-versa.

  • Nothing to install — it opens like any website; only an internet connection is needed.
  • Same login — the phone number and password they use in the app.
  • Built-in help — a Bangla user guide is at …/app/guide (also reachable from the web app's ⚙ Settings → সাহায্য / Help).

✓ Both apps, one panel

Whether a shopkeeper uses the phone app or the web, their shop shows up the same way in your admin panel — one subscription, one set of data.

15 Troubleshooting & FAQ

The panel looks out of date or a button does nothing

Your browser may be showing a cached copy. Do a hard refresh — Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) — to load the newest version.

I can't sign in

Confirm the username is exactly Admin and the password is correct. If a page briefly appears blank, the host's one-time security check is running — wait a moment and refresh once.

A shopkeeper says they can't log in or sync

Check their shop's Status and Expiry in Shops. If suspended, unsuspend them. If Expired, set a new expiry date. Both restore access instantly.

A shop I deleted still shows on someone's phone

Expected. Server deletes clean the cloud and panel; the phone keeps its local copy until the app is reinstalled. Ask them to reinstall for a clean slate.

The Outstanding / Sales totals look wrong

They usually reflect leftover test data. Use section 12's cleanup steps to remove test shops, junk customers, or demo sales, then check Overview again.

I forgot my admin password

There's no self-service reset — the setup page was removed for security. Recovery needs developer access to the server database. Keep your password backed up in a password manager to avoid this.
